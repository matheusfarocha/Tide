# tide · brand

**Idea:** the moon pulls the tide. Your attention moves the same way: it drifts out and comes back. TIDE makes that movement visible, then teaches you to feel it yourself.

**Tagline:** learn to feel your mind drift.

## Mark

A crescent moon over a single wave line (`public/icon.svg`, `<Mark />` in `src/components/ui.tsx`). Use it with the serif wordmark **tide**, always lowercase. Don't recolor the wave or put the mark on light backgrounds.

## Color

| Token | Hex | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Night | `#03050c` | The sky, the background |
| Shore | `#eaf3f7` | Text, the moon |
| Tide | `#7fe7ff` | Focus, primary accent, the waterline |
| Ember | `#ffb570` | Drifting. Only used to mean drift |
| Dusk | `#bea8ff` | Drowsy |
| Settle | `#b0c4e8` | Settling in |

State colors always mean a state. The water itself changes to the current one.

## Type

- **Instrument Serif:** the voice. Headlines, cues, anything TIDE "says". Italic for whispers.
- **Geist:** reading text.
- **Geist Mono:** data and labels. Lowercase, letter-spaced.

## Material: sea glass

Every surface is frosted glass floating on the tide: a soft white-to-cyan fill, a bright rim that catches the moon, and a 22px blur, so the water behind tints it. One material, no other card styles.

## Motion

The tide is always breathing (6 breaths/min when idle, your real breath in a session). Pages arrive with a tide sweep; content rises in one piece at a time. Everything respects reduced-motion.

## Voice

Calm, short, second person. "Your mind drifts. Learn to feel it." Never clinical, never medical, never guilt. Celebrate noticing, not stillness: "You caught it."

## Experience principles

1. **Eyes closed first.** Voice, sound and haptics carry the session; the screen is for before and after.
2. **One main action per screen.** Usually "hold to begin".
3. **Every touch answers.** Ripples, haptics, the tide responds.
4. **Built to be outgrown.** Coach support fades as you improve.
5. **Private by design.** Video stays on the phone; only numbers leave it.
6. **Accessible.** 44px targets, readable contrast, screen-reader labels, reduced motion.
